# OpenNV Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, ability, or level of experience.

Be respectful and specific; critique ideas rather than people; accept
responsibility for mistakes; protect private and security-sensitive
information; and respect contributor time and project scope. Harassment,
threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, intimidation, and
sustained disruption are unacceptable.

This code applies in project repositories, discussions, reviews, events, and
other spaces where someone represents OpenNV. Maintainers may remove content
and warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose conduct
is harmful.

Report platform abuse through GitHub's confidential abuse-reporting mechanism.
For project-community incidents, contact an OpenNV organization owner privately
and request a confidential channel. Do not publish sensitive incident details
in an issue. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is a violation.
